    How to play
    
    
      
  • Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the d-pad. Walk into a monster to attack it.
    • 
      
  • Q uses your class ability. P drinks a healing potion. I opens your pack. Space waits a turn.
    • 
      
  • In the pack, press 1–9 to use or equip an item, and Shift + number to drop it.
    • 
      
  • Find the > on each floor to go deeper. Gear: ) weapon, [ armor, ! potion, ? scroll, $ gold.
    • 
      
  • A merchant & sets up shop on some floors. Walk into them to buy gear, potions and upgrades, or sell spare gear.
    • 
      
  • Watch your step. Hidden traps ^ spike, poison, burn or set off alarms. Rogues spot them far more often.
    • 
      
  • Cracked walls ▒ hide secret rooms. Walk into one to break it. Chests = hold loot, though some bite back.
    • 
      
  • Poison and burning hurt every turn, and frost slows you. Potions cure poison and burns. Water puts out flames.
    • 
      
  • Partway down, a Stone Golem guards the stairs. When it raises its fists, step out of the glowing ring.
    • 
      
  • The Daily Run gives everyone the same dungeon, class and settings for the day. You get one try. Copy your result to compare with friends.
    • 
      
  • On the last floor the Amber Wyrm breathes fire in a line. When the floor glows ≈, step out of it.
    • 
      
  • Your run saves after every turn. Esc returns to the menu. B toggles music and M mutes all sound.
